How these figures are computed
  • Suspended accounts: profiles whose protective suspension is active at display time.
  • Applications pending: files at the To review, Under review, Technical test or Interview steps.
  • Unresolved support tickets: tickets Open, In progress or Waiting (Resolved and Closed excluded).
  • Late milestones: reference date passed and milestone still being processed (funded, in execution, under CP or client review, in revision or in dispute). Accepted milestones and those not started are excluded.
  • Active subscriptions: status Active or Renewal pending, with no end date or a future one. Expired: status Expired, or end date passed without renewal.
  • Evolution: creations counted per UTC day over the displayed window; each total is compared with the immediately preceding period of the same length.
