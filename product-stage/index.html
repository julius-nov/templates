Stage 02 — Editorial · The long listen

Silence, composed.

We started with a question no spec sheet answers: what does a room sound like when it stops existing? The answer took three years, six microphone arrays and one driver material borrowed from satellite optics.

01 — The driver

A 40 mm beryllium-coated diaphragm moves air like a concert hall moves a crowd: effortlessly, and all at once. Bass stays taut at 20 Hz; the top octave stays silk at 20 kHz.

02 — The quiet

Six microphones sample the room 48,000 times a second. The inverse wave they build cancels the world before your eardrum ever meets it — −42 dB of it, from jet engines to open-plan offices.
